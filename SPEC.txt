============================================================
RULES (defaults — label them "règles par défaut" in the UI)
============================================================

These are assumptions until one workshop corrects them.

- Sizes are OUTSIDE dimensions.
- Each caisson is its own box: 2 joues, top, bottom, back.
- Boxes sit flush. Gap between caissons = 0.
- Joues are full height. Top and bottom sit BETWEEN the joues.
- Carcass and shelves: 18 mm. Back: 8 mm.
- Shelf depth = depth − 20 mm.
- Back is a simple inset panel. No groove, no rebate.
  Cut list calls it "fond rapporté".
- Wall limits:
    width 600–6000 mm
    height 1800–2800 mm
    depth 300–700 mm
    socle 0–200 mm
    jeu plafond 0–300 mm
  Usable height = hauteur − socle − jeu.
- Caisson width: min 300 mm, max 1200 mm.
- At least one caisson.
- Sum of caisson widths ALWAYS equals wall width.

Reflow:
  Editing a width takes from or gives to the caisson on its right.
  The last caisson trades with the one on its left.
  If that would break 300 mm, refuse the edit.

Add:
  Split the widest caisson in half, only if both halves are ≥ 300 mm.
  Otherwise refuse.

Remove:
  Its width goes to the right neighbour, or to the left if it is the last.
  Cannot remove the final caisson.

============================================================
INTERIOR (per caisson)
============================================================

- Étagères: count 0–8, spaced evenly in the free zone.
  No free-dragged positions in v0.1.
- Tringle: aucune / haute / basse / double.
  Haute ≈ 80 mm down from the top of the usable interior.
  Basse ≈ half of usable interior.
  A rail is a part in the list, not a priced accessory.
- Tiroirs: count 0–6, stacked from the bottom, equal height, above the socle.
  Each drawer = a front + a simple box (2 sides, front, back, bottom).
  No slides, no Blum.
- Pantalonnière: yes/no. One pull-out in that column.
  One accessory line in the list, not a full mechanism.

============================================================
FRONT (one choice for the whole wall)
============================================================

- Aucune.
- Battantes: 1 door if caisson < 600 mm, 2 if ≥ 600 mm.
  Overlay on the box. Height = usable height.
- Coulissantes: 2 leaves if wall < 2400 mm, else 3.
  Leaves span the WALL, not each caisson.

============================================================
CUT LIST (human-readable, not CNC)
============================================================

One row per part:
  caisson number, role (joue, dessus, dessous, fond, étagère,
  porte, tiroir…), quantity, length, width, thickness, material, chants.
No kerf. No nesting. No grain. No drilling.
Sorted by caisson, then by role.

Sizing convention:
  Joues: height = usable height? Wait — full box height.
  Lock this:
  - Caisson outside: width W, height = usable height H, depth D.
  - Socle is a separate plinth under the boxes, not part of the caisson carcass.
    Plinth: one front rail (and simple returns) across the wall, height = socle.
    If socle is 0, no plinth parts.
  - 2 joues: H × D × 18
  - Dessus and dessous: (W − 2×18) × D × 18
  - Fond rapporté: (W − 2×18) × (H − 2×18) × 8
  - Étagère: (W − 2×18) × (D − 20) × 18
  - Door (battante): height H, width = W / doorCount (overlay, ignore gaps in v0.1)
  - Sliding leaf: height H, width = wallWidth / leafCount
  - Drawer front: width (W − 2×18), height = drawer zone / count
  - Drawer box: simple, 18 mm, depth D − 50 mm. Bottom 8 mm.
  - Rail: length = W − 2×18, listed as "tringle" with diameter note 25 mm, not a panel.
